    How iconprism works

    1. Draw. Pick a tool on the left (rectangle, circle, star, pen, text) and drag on the artboard. To trace a sketch or logo, add it with Reference image in the top bar.
    2. Style. Select a shape and change its colour, gradient and outline in the panel on the right.
    3. Combine. Select two or more shapes (Shift+click) and use Union, Subtract, Intersect or Exclude to build new shapes.
    4. Check. Keep important parts inside the dashed guides. The previews show how phones will crop your icon.
    5. Export. Press Export icon set to download every size for iOS, Android and the web in one ZIP.

    While dragging

    • Shift keeps squares, circles and 45° lines, and keeps proportions when resizing.
    • Alt draws from the centre. In Edit points it moves one curve handle on its own.
    • ⌘ or Ctrl turns snapping off for that drag.
    • Double-click a shape to edit its points, or text to change the words.

    On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl in place of ⌘.
